## What the paper does
Applies the X-Means clustering algorithm (K-Means offshoot, model-order chosen
by BIC) to operation data of a water/silica-gel adsorption chiller (HVAC
component in building automation). It segments 2011 monitoring data into duty
cycles by the cooling-load variable Q7, clusters the return-temperature (T_LTre)
segments, and adds nMDS 2D projection with 95% confidence bands/ellipses. Goal:
automatic detection of system states / duty cycles for fault detection and
diagnosis (FDD) and, prospectively, energy-efficiency profiling.
