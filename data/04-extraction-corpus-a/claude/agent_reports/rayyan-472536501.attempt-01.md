## What the paper actually is

A methodological study that couples an "enhanced DEA" (CCR) efficiency method with
life-cycle analysis to evaluate and **rank 16 technological combinations**
(power-generation × electrolytic anode × aluminium-supply mode) for producing 1 ton of
aluminium in China, under 3 indicator sets (EPI / UEPI / TPI) and 4 policy scenarios.
Data are LCA reference values (GREET software + literature), not a real facility's
measured data. Purpose is explicitly a **decision-making method for technology
selection**.
