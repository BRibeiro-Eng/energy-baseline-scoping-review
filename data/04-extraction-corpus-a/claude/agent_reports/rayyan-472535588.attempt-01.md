## Reasoning (Stage 1)
The paper proposes a conceptual **framework** that integrates three management
systems (ISO 50001 EnMS + ISO 14001 EMS + ISO 14064 GHG verification), plus
technology-management practices and international-law obligations, so that
facilities can access carbon funds. Its contribution is management-system
integration and GHG monitoring/verification, not an energy performance
measurement model.

EnPI/EnB content is present but **incidental and generic**:
- Energy baseline described abstractly as "a reference point for the overall
  energy consumption of the organisation, which can be taken from a
  representative calendar year or a typical 12-month period" (p. 13).
- EnPIs described as "energy power indicators ... reflect overall as well as
  detailed energy efficiency, compared with the energy baseline, on a monthly
  basis" (p. 13).
- Generic mention of "variables that affect" energy points (people count,
  weather for HVAC) (p. 13).

None of these are tied together into an identifiable EnPI/EnB model: no
functional form, no defined variable set for a model, no construction/estimation
method. They are textbook ISO 50001 process steps. This triggers the v2.3
ineligible rules: "presents a tool, framework ... without any EnPI/EnB
measurement-model content" and "mentions EnPI/EnB only incidentally (no model
identified or characterised)."
