## Decision: methodologically INELIGIBLE

The paper's entire methodological content is multi-criteria decision-making
(MCDM): a two-stage approach combining FAHP (fuzzy AHP) for criteria weighting
with VIKOR, TOPSIS, WASPAS, and PROMETHEE to rank/prioritise 12 energy-
efficiency projects (EEPs) at an Egyptian petrochemical complex. FAHP criteria
weights are also compared across Egyptian, Irish, and Qatari firms.

critiqued. The evaluation "criteria" (implementation cost, energy cost saving,

reference list); ISO 50006 is not cited.
