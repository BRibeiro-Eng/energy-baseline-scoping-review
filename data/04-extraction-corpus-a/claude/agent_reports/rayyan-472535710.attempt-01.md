## Borderline note for human adjudication

The one non-trivial call is eligibility. The paper name-drops several
energy-quantification instruments — eeMeasure (EC tool), "IPMVP customized for
school environments", the Enerit Energy Flow Assessor, and IES "Building Energy
Index, Climate Energy Index and Carbon Assessor" (page 9) — and reports pilot
savings ranges (heating 19–48%, lighting, eeMeasure ~53% potential) and Green
Design simulated savings (64–113%). None of these is developed into a
characterised EnPI/EnB model in-text: they are cited as named validation
utilities and headline results, with no variables, functional form, or
construction detail. Under the v2.3 threshold ("identify and characterise the
model") this reads as incidental mention → ineligible. If a reviewer treats a
named-but-uncharacterised index (e.g. "Building Energy Index") plus an
IPMVP-customised M&V approach as sufficient EnPI content, eligibility could flip;
flagged here so the human can adjudicate. Even if flipped, the case stays
level_3b=FALSE (school buildings).
