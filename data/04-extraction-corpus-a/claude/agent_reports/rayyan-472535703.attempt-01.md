## Reasoning — eligibility (consequential; flag for blind-first review)

evaluation method** for selecting automatic-identification (barcode / RFID /
biometric) systems in logistics/warehousing. Its model (Sect. 2.2, eqs. 1–10)
scores five *information* characteristics — up-to-dateness, relevancy,
