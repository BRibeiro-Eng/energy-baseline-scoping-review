**Paper:** Wang, E.-Z. & Pan, T. (2025). "Does ISO 50001 adoption reduce
manufacturing energy intensity? Micro-evidence from China." *Economic Analysis
and Policy* 86, 653–672. DOI 10.1016/j.eap.2025.03.050. 20 pp, clean text
(pymupdf; title match 100, DOI found in text).

**What the paper is:** a firm-level econometric policy-evaluation study. It uses
propensity-score-matching difference-in-differences (PSM-DID) on a panel of
Chinese manufacturing firms (ISO 50001 certification records 2011–2021 matched to
China Annual Tax Survey 2007–2016) to estimate the causal effect of ISO 50001
(ISOEnMS) certification on firm energy intensity (~26% reduction), plus mechanism
tests (equipment investment, cheaper external financing).
