## Reasoning

The paper is a life-cycle-assessment / cost / CO₂e comparison of renovation
strategies for **one typical Estonian apartment building** (24 apartments, built 1986,
renovated 2021). Building energy performance is computed by **IDA-ICE dynamic
simulation**; the analysis axes are life-cycle cost (global-cost / NPV), carbon
footprint (Estonian LCA method, One-Click LCA), and total energy demand.

Ineligible on two independent, converging grounds:

1. **Registered exclusion — "residential buildings without industrial component."**
   The exclusive focus is a residential apartment building; there is no industrial /
   organisational energy-management dimension.
2. **No EnPI/EnB measurement model is presented.** The term "EnPI" appears, but only
   as a **building energy-intensity indicator, kWh/(m²a)** (Table 2: 178 before / 123
   after) used as a comparison axis. There is no energy-baseline model, no
   normalisation-variable/regression model, no SEC fit. Normative framing is **EPBD +
   LCA (ISO 14040, EN 15978, Level(s))**, not ISO 50001/50006 energy management. The
   single "energy management" phrase (p.3) is a generic definitional aside.

`paper_type = single applied case`: one reference building, one site.
`level_3b = false`: residential building, not a continuous-process industry
(forced to `false` + `"not applicable"` by the ineligibility rule, and correct on the
merits).

## Borderline flag for human adjudication (Tier-1)

This is a **genuine borderline eligibility call** worth blind-first human review. The
paper explicitly *defines and uses* an EnPI ("The Energy Performance Indicator (EnPI)

not a characterised measurement model. Confidence 0.75 reflects that tension.
