## Reasoning for the ineligibility call (strict per v2.3 bar)
The paper is a case report on a hybrid PV-wind + battery (BESS) installation at the
administrative offices building of the Municipality of Valderice (Italy). Its substantive
content is (1) a system/prototype description, (2) one year of production monitoring vs.
expected, (3) four deliberately **simple benefit indexes** — energy-saving (EnS),
economic-saving (EcS), CO2-reduction (CO2R), power-peak-reduction (PPR) — and (4) a
comparison of two BESS control logics (System Led vs Market Led) under Time-of-Use tariffs
via MATLAB simulation, judged by economic savings.

These indexes are generation/self-sufficiency and economic/emissions *benefit* metrics
(e.g. EnS = 1 − Ea/Eb, demand vs. on-site production), **not** EnPI/EnB
energy-performance-measurement models in the ISO 50006 sense: there is no normalisation of
energy consumption against relevant driving variables, no energy baseline, and no
SEC/regression model. Per the v2.3 bar and the closed Stage-2/3 model taxonomy, this reads
as a system-design / energy-management (battery-control) study rather than the presentation,
proposal, evaluation, or critique of an energy-performance measurement model. It also aligns
with the prompt's INELIGIBLE class "purely economic energy management without performance
measurement."

**Disambiguation flagged for the adjudicator:** the paper's repeated token "ENPI" is the EU
**European Neighbourhood & Partnership Instrument** funding programme (Italy-Tunisia
DE.DU.ENER.T. cooperation project) — **NOT** "Energy Performance Indicator." A keyword
detector or reviewer could mis-fire on this; it is not evidence of an EnPI model.
