## Rationale

The paper is a **questionnaire survey of ISO 50001 EnMS implementation status in Italy**.
It reports, from 40 certified companies, 14 certification bodies, and consultants:
(i) background motivations for adopting an EnMS; (ii) which ISO 50001 requirements are
hardest to satisfy; (iii) elements most often found non-compliant; and (iv) cumulative
energy savings achieved. It also presents certification-uptake statistics (Italy vs.
Germany) from the ISO Survey 2013.

EnPIs and the energy baseline appear **only as ISO 50001 requirements** whose definition
companies find difficult, plus a generic, reference-attributed definition of EnPI types

Mancò [18], Goldstein et al. [19]). The paper **presents/proposes/evaluates/critiques no
