## Rationale
The paper is a survey / program-evaluation study: it reports findings from 40 live polling
questions posed to 54 participants from 28 companies during a virtual training on the DOE
50001 Ready program, characterising EnMS implementation barriers and readiness.

EnPI/EnB content appears only **incidentally**:
- Task T11 ("Energy Performance Indicators and Energy Baselines") is one of the 25 listed
  50001 Ready Navigator tasks (p.3).
- Poll topics on normalising energy data with relevant variables (p.6, p.11).
- Linear regression cited as a method 50001 Ready recommends and that ~47% of participants
  were "considering" using (p.11, p.13), and listed as an area where more training is needed.

No EnPI/EnB model is identified or characterised — no specific variables, functional form,
or construction/estimation approach for any model. This matches the v2.3 ineligibility
clauses "mentions EnPI/EnB only incidentally (no model identified or characterised)" and
"presents a tool, framework ... without any EnPI/EnB measurement-model content" (the 50001
Ready Navigator being that framework).
