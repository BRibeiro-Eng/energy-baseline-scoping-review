## What the paper does
Derives 35 Energy Performance Indicators (EnPIs) for multifamily **residential**
buildings from a literature review, organised under the six IEA (2013) factors
(climate, building envelope, building services/energy systems, O&M, occupant
behaviour, indoor environmental quality). It then runs an expert ranking survey
(280 contacted, 106 valid responses) and analyses the rankings with Kendall's
Coefficient of Concordance and the Kruskal–Wallis H test to produce a prioritised
EnPI listing for retrofit planning. No empirical building-energy dataset/case is
charted; the empirical object is expert opinion.
