## What the paper is
Single applied case: an energy baseline / M&V reference model for one plastic
injection molding plant (Casablanca, Morocco) producing electrical components.
Dependent variable = electrical energy consumption (kWh); single independent
variable = production (metallic parts, tonnes). 29 monthly observations. Three
modelling methods compared: simple linear regression (Y = 28.54·x1 + 177,362),
ANN (feed-forward, MATLAB), and ANFIS. Reported fit: Regression R²=53.44%,
ANFIS R²=69.26%, ANN R²=97.86% / CV(RMSE)=7.70%.
