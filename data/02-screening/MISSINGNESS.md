## Interpretation

Retrieval was **differential according to observed variables** (Rayyan decision, DOI availability, year, document type): retrieval succeeded far more often for records with a DOI (91.6%) than without (30.3%), and more often for Included (85.0%) than Maybe (65.4%) records — the missing set is dominated by DOI-less records and conference proceedings, i.e. records that were harder to retrieve; their full-text eligibility is unobserved (never screened), so no eligibility rate can be asserted for them. The sector-signal scan above identifies the OBSERVABLE candidates only — records whose titles carry a continuous-process term. It does not bound the number of Corpus-B-relevant records among the non-retrieved: a record whose title lacks a sector term but whose full text concerns a continuous-process sector is invisible to this scan. The listed titles are candidates, not an upper bound, and they must be acknowledged as a limitation in the PRISMA flow ('reports not retrieved') rather than silently dropped. All counts are reproducible from `articles.csv` + the frozen PDF store via this script.
