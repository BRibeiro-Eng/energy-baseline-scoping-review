# Corpus B ⊂ Corpus A — decision of 2026-09-17

**Decision-maker:** reviewer (Bernardo Ribeiro). **Status:** applied.

## The problem
`output/corpus_profile.rosters()` computed A and B independently. Two reports ended up in B without being in A, because the blind Tier-1 form (`production/reviewer_workspace/reviews/membership/blind/`) accepted the incoherent pair `level_3b = true` + `methodological_eligibility = false`, both adjudicated by the reviewer:

| key | article | methodological eligibility | level_3b |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rayyan-472535628` | Pelser, Vosloo, Mathews (2017), cement, South Africa | **false** (adjudicated) | true (adjudicated) |
| `rayyan-472535750` | Wu et al. (2014), glass / distribution centre | **false** (adjudicated) | true (adjudicated) |

By design (protocol §5.3), B is a charting partition within A: `level_3b` has no meaning for a report that failed eligibility.

## The decision
The eligibility verdict — taken blind, before any Stage C extraction — stands, and the rule B ⊂ A is applied. **Both leave B.** Eligibility was not reopened: doing so after seeing what each record contributes to the synthesis would be deciding membership by content.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Corpus A | 331 | 331 |
| Exclusions for methodological eligibility | 117 | 117 |
| Corpus B | 42 | **40** |
| Charted in Stage C | 41 | **39** (+1 in quarantine, `rayyan-472536714`) |
| Bibliometric view A∪B | 333 | no longer exists (= A) |

- `output/corpus_profile.py`: `enforce_b_subset_a()` removes the two decided reports from B and **stops the pipeline** on any other report in B∖A without a recorded decision. Tests: `output/test_corpus_membership.py` (3, including A = 331, B = 40 on the real data).
- Stage C records moved, not deleted: `production/stage_c/claude/outputs/` → `production/stage_c/claude/outside_B/`; forms and dossiers → `output/forms/_fora_de_B/`, `output/dossiers/_fora_de_B/`; `output/forms/INDEX.md` annotated.

- `stage_c/PERFIL_CORPUS_B_2026-09-17.md` regenerated over 39 records by `stage_c/perfil_corpus_b.py` (the generator reproduces the profile of 16/09 exactly when run over the 41: `stage_c/_arquivo_perfil_reproducao_41_registos_2026-09-17.md`).
