# Manuscript figures

TikZ sources of the figures of the article, as compiled with the MDPI LaTeX template (`\input{figures/style}` loads the palette and TikZ libraries). Values are those of the article's tables and of `data/06-late-verification/`. Only `make_forest.py` computes anything: the exact Clopper–Pearson intervals of Fig. 5, from the counts written in the script; it writes `fig-checks-forest.tex` (Python 3 with SciPy).

| File | Figure |
|---|---|
| `fig-chain.tex` | Fig. 1 — Energy measurement chain (ISO 50001 → ISO 50006 → ISO 50015) and its conditional link to emission-reduction estimates |
| `fig-prisma.tex` | Fig. 2 — PRISMA-ScR flow, with the later repeat decisions (D16) as a dashed branch |
| `fig-evidence-chain.tex` | Fig. 3 — Reported evidence along the measurement chain: Corpus B shares by reporting consideration, and the 13 refinery and petrochemical publications |
| `fig-mechanisms-diverging.tex` | Fig. 4 — Corpus B mechanisms by effect on the model and claim status (general vs. the study's own model) |
| `fig-checks-forest.tex` | Fig. 5 — Results of the checks of the amendment of 16 October 2026 (selection, Corpus B values, Corpus A audit) with exact 95% intervals; generated by `make_forest.py` |
