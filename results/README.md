# Results

- `corpus-a/`: tables (CSV) and figures (PDF) of the registered bibliometric run over Corpus A (331 publications), with `catalog.en.json` (English title and caption of every figure), `run_manifest.json` (inputs and their hashes), `package_versions.json` and `prisma_ledger.json`. Table labels are in English; the figure files keep the Portuguese labels of the registered run (mapping in `docs/data-dictionary.md` §6).
- `corpus-b/`: the Corpus B aggregates used by the article (`tabelas_cap3_2026-09-18.json`), the BibTeX of the 39 charted reports, the evidence map, and two working analyses (`PERFIL_CORPUS_B_2026-09-17.md`, the extractor's reading of each record; `ANALISE_CONTEUDO_2026-09-15.md`, with English translation). The aggregates precede the October 2026 corrections; the article applies those corrections (`data/06-late-verification/`).
- `manuscript-figures/`: TikZ sources of the figures of the article.
